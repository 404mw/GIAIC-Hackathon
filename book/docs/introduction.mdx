# Introduction

Welcome to "Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics"!

This book is a comprehensive guide to the exciting world of physical artificial intelligence and humanoid robotics. We will explore the history, current state, and future of this rapidly evolving field.

## What to Expect

In this book, you will learn about:

*   The fundamentals of AI and robotics
*   The challenges of creating autonomous systems that can interact with the physical world
*   The latest advancements in humanoid robot design and technology
*   The ethical implications of creating intelligent machines

We hope you enjoy this journey into the future of technology.
